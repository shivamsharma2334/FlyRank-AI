Modern, creative, clean, professional, and approachable.

04. Logo / Favicon

Personal Logo

A simple monogram using my initials. The rounded shape represents a modern, friendly, and professional identity.

05. Style Note

My visual identity is modern, clean, and professional. Poppins is used for headings and Inter for body text, supported by a purple and teal color palette on a light background. The overall design focuses on simplicity, clarity, and creativity.

06. Design Rules

✓ Do

Use consistent fonts, colors, spacing, rounded corners, and clean layouts.

✕ Don't

Avoid too many colors, unnecessary fonts, cluttered layouts, and excessive decoration.
